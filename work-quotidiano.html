The Line

"Quotidiano — the everyday. Soft where the VOLT Collection was sharp, and just as intentional."

Where the runway line is armor, Quotidiano is the everyday counterpart of Pimpinicchio New York — a study in restraint. Slouchy, unlined forms in olive and russet vegan suede, cut to carry a real day and a laptop with it, then finished with the studio's signature hardware and a single drop of volt-yellow.

Each piece is named for the hills above Spoleto, in Umbria, where I grew up: the Rocca fortress, the Monteluco wood, the olive groves. Designed in New York, rooted in Italy — engineered end to end, from first sketch to production-ready specification.

02The Rocca Tote
Named for La Rocca Albornoziana, the fortress above Spoleto

Not because it's hard — because it holds everything. A soft, unlined tote in vegan suede with black bio-based vegan leather trim, foldable flat for shipping, on a studded base with integrated piercing rings. It carries a whole day, a 16-inch laptop included, and comes with two removable pouches on dog-clips. 46.6 × 35 × 16 cm. Russet brown. Brass-gold and gunmetal hardware.

03The Monteluco Shoulder
Named for the sacred wood above Spoleto

The green mountain I grew up escaping to. A soft, slouchy shoulder bag in olive vegan suede that drapes effortlessly without losing its edge — a free-moving T-bar handle plus a detachable strap, a studded base, side D-rings, and the signature volt-yellow logo patch. Built for every day, carried two ways, sized to close cleanly around a 13-inch MacBook. 34 × 21 × 11 cm. Dark olive, Pantone 19-0419. Brass-gold and gunmetal hardware.
