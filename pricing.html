Questions.

Do I need a credit card to start? +
No. Spark is free forever — no card required. You only need payment details when you upgrade to Plus or Studio.
What does the AI actually do? +
PMPNY Intelligence analyzes fashion trends, reads brand websites and Shopify stores automatically, analyzes product images, and gives you actionable commercial direction — colors, silhouettes, market position, collection gaps. Think of it as a senior creative director available 24/7.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes. No contracts, no minimum commitment. Cancel from your account settings and you keep access until the end of your billing period.
What's the difference between Plus and Studio? +
Plus gives you unlimited access to the AI platform. Studio adds Francesco's personal attention — quarterly reviews of your actual collection with written strategic feedback delivered as a PDF. It's the difference between using the tool and having a studio partner.
Can the AI analyze my competitor's website? +
Yes. Paste any URL in the chat and the AI reads it automatically — including Shopify stores, which return full product data including prices, materials, and descriptions. Works on any plan.
How accurate are the trend signals? +
Trend signals are generated monthly by Claude based on runway data, resale market signals, and street style trends. They reflect current commercial momentum — what's selling, what's rising, what's peaking. Updated automatically every month.
