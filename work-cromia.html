The Mandate

"Nothing arrived on my desk half-finished. I designed the collections from zero — every shape, every clasp, every stud."

LA.I.PE SpA is the Italian manufacturing house behind some of the industry's most exacting names — Vivienne Westwood, Valextra, Berluti. From 2017 to 2019, across four seasons, I designed for Cromia — a house identified, in its own words, by the richness and opulence of its collections, with metal fittings that characterize and differentiate the numerous lines of each season.

That made hardware the heart of the work. I originated silhouettes from a blank page and engineered the metalwork that set each line apart, carrying both from the first pencil line to the final plated clasp. Not styling, and not a contribution to someone else's concept — full authorship, shape and hardware, at the standard of a house trusted by those names.

Design DNA — Codes That Evolve

One house language,
carried across bags.

The Cromia codes were built to travel. The Uptown bag's round handle is the evolution of the Cromia "C"; its centered macro-stud closure descends from the IT bag — the same detail that became Precious. Shape, hardware, and monogram were developed as a system, not one-off bags.

Signature Collections & Lines

Across four seasons I originated the lines that carried Cromia — each from concept and silhouette through print and hardware. These became the recognizable codes of the house.

FW 2019/20 → SS20 · Signature Line
Ginger
The most performing line of the season and the queen of the FW19 collection, carried into SS20. A clean shape concealing bellows that make it functional and elegant, defined by a geometric tuck closure and pyramid studs in burnished gold.
Spring / Summer 2020 · IT Bag
Precious
Born from the identifying details of the Cromia IT bag, distilled into a macro-stud motif — a single bold hardware gesture carrying a minimal, elegant shape.
Spring / Summer 2020 · Essential Line
Kiki
An essential line following Ginger — refined details, highlighted stitching, and micro-stud light points, crowned by a closure of full-and-empty play that reveals the leather beneath.
Spring / Summer 2019 · Collection Face
Nubia
The line that represents the collection: rich in accessories yet clean in form, enlivened by contrast inking, and closed with the colored-enamel "C" monogram that shifts with each side color.
Seasonal · Prints & Statement
IT
The house's print vehicle, re-enriched each season — abstract overlapped leathers, fruity marble textures, safari pop prints. It grew into IT Becca (laser drilling, micro studs, pearls) and IT Trendy, five mini bags with removable fur.
Hardware — Engineered from Zero

Closures with character.

Two signature closures anchored the collections. The Square Gold Lock — a geometric, refined lock built to give clean shapes character, used across finishes through FW19 and SS20 — and the Square Dog Hock, a square carabiner with a new open-and-close mechanism engineered to make shoulder bags secure to wear. Both taken from dimensioned CAD through hand study to plated brass.

Recognition

Built to a red-carpet standard.

Pieces from these collections reached the red carpet at the Venice Film Festival, produced to the standard of a house trusted by Vivienne Westwood, Valextra, and Berluti. Two years of designing at that bar built the technical fluency — silhouette, hardware, and production — that underpins every project the studio takes on today.
