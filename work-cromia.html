The Mandate

"Nothing arrived on my desk half-finished. I designed the collections from zero — every shape, every clasp, every stud."

LA.I.PE SpA is the Italian manufacturing house behind some of the industry's most exacting names — Vivienne Westwood, Valextra, Berluti. Working to that technical standard, I held full design authorship over Cromia's seasonal lines: originating every silhouette from a blank page, engineering the hardware suite that defined it, and carrying both through to production.

This was not styling, and it was not contribution to someone else's concept. It was end-to-end authorship — the shape and the hardware, both mine — across 17 to 25 handbag collections a year, from the first pencil line to the final plated clasp.

Full Authorship — Sketch to Production

Every Cromia bag travelled the same road: a hand sketch, resolved into 3D mechanical CAD, prototyped with the atelier, corrected across samples, and released to production. Owning the whole chain meant the idea never got diluted in translation — the clasp on the shelf was the clasp I drew.

Signature Lines & Hardware

Across two years, certain designs became recognizable codes of the house — each one originated end to end, shape and hardware together.

Hardware Suite
Ginger
A bespoke hardware family — clasp, rings, and fittings — designed from mold to plating finish and carried across a season's silhouettes as a unifying signature.
Seasonal Line
Kiki
A full seasonal line originated from zero — silhouette, proportion, construction, and its own hardware language — developed from first sketch through to production sampling.
Construction
Matelassé
Quilted, padded matelassé bodies engineered to hold architectural structure while keeping a soft hand — a technique refined across multiple collections.
Recognition

Built to a red-carpet standard.

Pieces from these collections reached the red carpet at the Venice Film Festival, produced to the standard of a house trusted by Vivienne Westwood, Valextra, and Berluti. Two years of designing at that bar built the technical fluency — silhouette, hardware, and production — that underpins every project the studio takes on today.
